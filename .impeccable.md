## Design Context

### Users
People co-building a product with Codex, including users with consulting, analysis, and business planning experience who want to contribute judgment without monitoring code continuously. The companion lives in a macOS desktop corner and returns users to the original conversation when their decision matters.

### Brand Personality
Cute, calm, attentive. A chibi owl quietly accompanies real work; its chicks represent actual child agents. English is the default interface language, with a Chinese option.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the user-approved owl, readable eyes, book, brief progress spell, and yellow decision lantern. Keep progress above the owl and optional shared account usage below it. Use five gentle character accents with text identifiers. Match native macOS typography and controls; keep surrounding chrome restrained and adapt to light/dark appearance.

The owl silhouette should blend cleanly into the desktop without a pale outer stroke, white matte, or surrounding plate. Use restrained native glass only for the compact name, quota, and progress surfaces; respect Reduce Transparency. Keep quota freshness below the small quota pill as unboxed text, not inside a larger card. Planning, building, and checking should visibly read. New requests must promptly signal that the roadmap is being reviewed before a newly confirmed plan is shown.

### Design Principles
- Show little by default; reveal the roadmap and settings on demand.
- When the roadmap is hidden, use small overhead dots: green completed, deep green current, gray pending, yellow decision, red required input. Keep them compact and clickable; short orbit motion accompanies actual completion. Required information is entered in the original destination, not the Pet.
- Each conversation owns its state. Color complements a readable conversation name.
- Only actual progress should trigger celebration; animation never invents progress or approves a decision.
- Preserve explicit visibility choices. One default quota display, optional copies, no automatic reopening.
- Respect reduced motion and keyboard access; keep controls readable when the owl scales.
